Why this equipment

This equipment covers the full chain of a drone unit’s work in the field:

  • heavy-lift drones for strikes, logistics, and equipment evacuation;
  • power supply (generators and EcoFlow units) to keep drone batteries and gear running non-stop;
  • Starlink terminals and routers for stable communication between crews and command;
  • a controller and tablets for piloting and battlefield monitoring through special apps;
  • and a monitor for real-time video feed and navigation.

 

Together, these tools let the crew operate independently in the field — communicate, coordinate, and carry out missions without depending on unreliable infrastructure.

Who’s behind this campaign

Preston Stewart

Former US Army artillery officer and content creator who covers the war in Ukraine and other global conflicts. He has previously worked with Dzyga’s Paw and other organizations to deliver vital aid to Ukrainian defenders.

Dzyga’s Paw

A Ukrainian charity connecting the international community with frontline defenders. Since 2022, we have delivered high-tech equipment directly to combat units, raising over $7,000,000 for Ukraine’s defense.

The 52nd International Assault Regiment “Forge” of the 1st Corps of the National Guard of Ukraine “Azov” brings together fighters from 40 countries, fighting side by side with Ukrainians for freedom. Until recently known as the Azov International Battalion, it grew into a regiment after a year and a half of active combat. Regardless of their country of origin, each fighter is drawn to Azov’s professionalism and discipline, whose standards remain unchanged for foreign fighters.

Forge fighters have gained combat experience in the Toretsk and Dobropillia sectors and made their mark on Azov’s history by capturing 18 prisoners in 24 hours during a single combat mission.
